# Brief: Pintertag (iOS)

Pintertag is a feed planner for Instagram creators and small brands. There is no account or login; everything is stored on the device and works offline.

Explore like a new user, in your persona. Try to reach these goals:
1. First launch: tap "Start with your photos" and add photos from the library (if you refuse photo access, check the app explains what to do and still works).
2. Grid tab: drag to swap two posts, tap a post and crop it. Turn on "What goes where".
3. Switch Brand / Personal and check the two grids stay separate.
4. Open the # button (Tools): Carousel & shoot (score a carousel, best of your shoot), Your Instagram grid (score from a screenshot), Booth (camera; refusing camera access must not break anything), Fits my grid.
5. Calendar tab: give a post a day and time (a notification permission prompt may appear; try refusing it).
6. Settings: find privacy, terms, support, and "Your data" (download / delete data on this device).

Report anything that blocks, confuses, is cut off, overlaps, is unreadable at your text size, looks broken on your screen size (especially iPad and small iPhones), or would make an App Store reviewer reject the app (crashes, dead buttons, placeholder text, features that don't work, permission prompts without a reason).
